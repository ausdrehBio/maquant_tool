For the same raw file our LFQ values differ from the deposited ones by a residual SD of **0.157** (log2), 0.44 times the difference between two replicates (0.354 in the deposited table, 0.338 in ours). Raw Intensity agrees less closely, but also better than replicates do. Outlier files: none. The 9 remeasured runs (REM) show somewhat larger residuals than the others (median 0.206 vs 0.151), still well below the replicate yardstick, plausibly because the two searches align these later measurements differently.

The scale differs: protein offsets have an SD of 0.89 log2 and the median sample shift is -1.85 log2 (our LFQ values are lower throughout). Per condition, the mean shift varies by 0.056 log2 (from AFMG to mock; one-way ANOVA p = 2.0e-05); for raw Intensity by only 0.010. So LFQ normalisation over a different run set moves the conditions slightly against each other, by up to 7% of the paper's fold-change threshold; the measurement itself is unaffected. Part B can confirm the cause.

The paper's statistics are reproduced exactly: largest deviation of log10 p 7.6e-13 for the t tests, and every call identical. The tests turn out to be the coefficient t test of a per-protein linear model with mock as reference (35 residual df) for strain vs mock, and a z test, as `multcomp::glht` uses for glm objects, for the G vs R pairs; adjusted p values are Benjamini–Hochberg within each comparison. The authors computed the z-test p values as 2·(1 − Φ(|z|)), which loses precision below about 1e-14 (values become multiples of 2.2e-16, then 0). Computed that way, ours match to 2.6e-13 and give the same zeros (RSIX 14, RFMX 95, AFMX 122). The suite itself uses the precise tail function; no call depends on this.

## Limitations

- The published table (Supplementary Data 5) comes from a MaxQuant search that is not deposited (versioned Ensembl IDs, fewer missing values). It is compared at the level of the published analysis; the deposited proteinGroups serves as the authors' baseline.
- Our search used the paper's 42 runs, the deposited one all 67. Until Part B (MaxQuant 2.9.0 on all 67 runs) has run, the version effect cannot be separated from the run-set effect.
- Imputation is random; results are medians and ranges over the seeds, and published outcomes that depend on the authors' single draw are reported, not judged (Q8).
- The GO:0051607 set and the gene symbols come from a dated annotation snapshot (annotation/), not from the authors' annotation version.
- The strains carry different NS1 sequences while the database holds only wild-type PR8 NS1, so NS1 quantities partly reflect peptide coverage (Q2, Q8).
