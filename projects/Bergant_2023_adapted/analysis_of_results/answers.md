## Limitations

- Decoy counts in this class are small (8 before filtering), so the decoy ratio per level is a rough estimate.
- The isobaric-twin check compares unmodified masses with parts of viral proteins and with identified canonical peptides; spectra explained by a modified or non-tryptic human peptide are not excluded. The spectra themselves (msms.txt) have not been inspected.
- Canonical means the databases of the search: a human sequence missing from Ensembl GRCh37 (a variant or a newer isoform) can look DPR-specific; this mostly concerns peptides without a viral anchor.
- The origin classes come from comparisons with the PR8 protein sequences only; the position of each DPR's junction is not in the FASTA header.
